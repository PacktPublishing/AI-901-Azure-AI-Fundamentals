# BrezyWeather Customer Knowledge Base

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is BrezyWeather?
BrezyWeather is a weather intelligence platform providing real-time forecasts, alerts, and climate insights for consumers and businesses.

### How do I access my forecast?
1. Visit brezyweather.com or open the mobile app
2. Enter your location or enable location services
3. View current conditions, hourly forecast, and 10-day outlook

### What alert types are available?
- Severe Thunderstorm Warning
- Tornado Warning
- Winter Storm Warning
- Heat Advisory
- Flood Watch
- Fog Advisory
- Wind Advisory

### How do I receive alerts?
Alerts are sent via:
- Push notifications (mobile app)
- SMS text messages
- Email notifications
- In-app banner alerts

### What should I do during a severe thunderstorm?
1. Move indoors immediately
2. Stay away from windows
3. Avoid using phones or electrical devices
4. Listen to local emergency broadcasts
5. Do not go outside until the warning expires

### How accurate are your forecasts?
- Next 24 hours: 90% accuracy
- 3-7 days: 80% accuracy
- 10+ days: 70% accuracy
Accuracy decreases with time and severe weather conditions.

### What does "wind advisory" mean?
Strong winds expected (30-40 mph gusts). Secure loose outdoor items. Avoid high bridges. Pedestrians may experience difficulty.
