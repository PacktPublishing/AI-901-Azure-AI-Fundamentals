# BrezyWeather Alert Protocols

## Alert Severity Levels

### Level 1: Advisory
**Definition:** Weather condition may pose a hazard but not immediately life-threatening.
**Examples:** Dense Fog, Wind Advisory, Lake Effect Snow Watch
**Customer Action:** Prepare; adjust travel plans; monitor conditions

### Level 2: Watch
**Definition:** Conditions are favorable for severe weather to develop; be prepared.
**Examples:** Severe Thunderstorm Watch, Tornado Watch, Flood Watch
**Customer Action:** Monitor alerts closely; have a plan; move to safe location if needed
**Duration:** Typically 4-8 hours

### Level 3: Warning
**Definition:** Severe weather has been observed or is imminent; take action immediately.
**Examples:** Tornado Warning, Severe Thunderstorm Warning, Flash Flood Warning
**Customer Action:** Take shelter immediately; stay indoors; avoid travel
**Duration:** Typically 30 minutes to 2 hours

## Watch vs. Warning (Key Difference)
- **Watch:** "Conditions are right for severe weather to develop"
- **Warning:** "Severe weather is happening or about to happen—take action NOW"

## Response Protocol by Alert Type

### Tornado Warning
1. Move to interior room on lowest floor
2. Stay away from windows
3. Cover yourself with mattress or blankets
4. Do not leave the building
5. Expected to last 15-60 minutes

### Severe Thunderstorm Warning
1. Move indoors
2. Stay away from windows
3. Avoid showers or baths
4. Unplug electrical devices
5. Expected to last 30-90 minutes

### Winter Storm Warning
1. Prepare emergency supplies (food, water, medications)
2. Avoid travel if possible
3. Check on elderly neighbors
4. Keep car fueled to at least half-tank
5. Expected to last 12-48 hours

## Customer Communication Guidelines
- Use clear, action-oriented language
- Specify the affected area (county/region)
- Include expected duration
- Provide safety recommendations
- Include update frequency (e.g., "Updated every 15 minutes")
